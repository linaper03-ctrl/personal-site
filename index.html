Red Light Green Light

An Interactive Installation Game (2026)

By Lina Persson and Klara Johansson, interaction design students at Södertörn University.

Red Light Green Light is an interactive two-player game inspired by the TV series Squid Game. Our vision was to create an immersive, fun experience where the physical body was used as a part of the installation. Just like in the series, the goal of Red Light Green Light is to move across the playing field, running when a doll sings and standing still when she's silent. With a web camera and an ml5 (MoveNet) body tracking model, the game registers human bodies, their physical location and movements to eliminate moving players when the doll is silent. Audio, different colors of lights via WebSocket and visuals presented on a monitor give the players feedback on the state of the game. To play, both players must lift their hands above their heads. The player who first reaches and presses the button without getting eliminated wins.
